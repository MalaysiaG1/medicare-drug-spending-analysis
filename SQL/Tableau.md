### Key Dashboard Takeaways
- **Gross Program Scale:** In 2022, gross Medicare Part D spend reached **$216B** across **1.19B claims** and **80.4M aggregate drug-beneficiary encounters**, averaging **$182.19 per prescription claim**.
- **Specialty Financial Burden:** High-cost orphan and specialty biologics demonstrate extreme spend concentration, exceeding **$600K–$1.3M+ average annual spend per beneficiary** despite treating small patient cohorts (<100 beneficiaries).
- **Utilization Intensity & Clinical Governance:** **Clozapine** demonstrated the highest utilization intensity (**80.65 claims per beneficiary**), reflecting weekly dispensing restrictions mandated by the FDA REMS absolute neutrophil count (ANC) safety monitoring program.
